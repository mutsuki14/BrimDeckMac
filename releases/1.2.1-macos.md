BrimDeck 的第一个 macOS 版本，对应 Windows 原版 1.2.1。

- 屏幕顶部的灵动岛：刘海、胶囊、指示条。移上去展开，移开收起。
- AI 用量、音乐和系统监控。用量、主题和设置与原版共用同一套核心。
- 「音乐」和 Spotify。手动填写的密钥保存在登录钥匙串。
- 提供 Apple 芯片（arm64）和 Intel（x64）两个 DMG。打开后把 BrimDeck 拖进「应用程序」。应用未签名，若被拦截，在「系统设置 → 隐私与安全性」里选择仍要打开。

<!-- lang: en-US -->
First macOS release of BrimDeck, matching Windows 1.2.1.

- A Dynamic Island at the top of the screen: notch, capsule and indicator. Move the pointer over it to open, and away to close.
- AI usage, music and the system monitor. Quotas, themes and settings use the same core as the original.
- The Music app and Spotify. Keys you enter are stored in the login keychain.
- Apple silicon (arm64) and Intel (x64) disk images. Open the DMG and drag BrimDeck into Applications. The app is unsigned; if macOS blocks it, allow it under System Settings → Privacy & Security.
